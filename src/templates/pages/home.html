Raluca-Maria Sandu

I am an AI and machine learning engineer and researcher focused on solving real-world problems. My work spans computer vision, multimodal systems, and generative AI, with a particular interest in building practical solutions for complex image and language problems. I got my PhD in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Bern in 2022, where I developed medical imaging methods for improving the evaluation of liver cancer treatments. That work was later integrated into a commercial product at a Swiss medical device company. I then spent three years at Accenture, where I delivered AI solutions across different industries and worked at the boundary between research, engineering, and business needs. Across consulting and freelance work, I have built systems ranging from medical imaging pipelines to computer vision and multimodal AI applications on AWS cloud. I am based in Zurich, Switzerland. Outside work, I am happiest outdoors, whereas is surfing, skiing, snowboarding, gardening, or birdwatching. I care deeply about nature, animals, and environmental conservation.
